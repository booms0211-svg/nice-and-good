PRIVACY POLICY

We respect your privacy. This service is designed to be used anonymously without the need to provide any personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. The birth date and time you enter are used solely for the purpose of calculating the Saju result within your browser session. This information is not stored, transmitted, or shared with any server or third party.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

This service does not use cookies for tracking. We may use your browser's local storage to save your preferred theme settings (e.g., light or dark mode), but this information is stored only on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The Saju calculation is performed by a JavaScript library that runs entirely within your browser. No data is sent to external APIs for the calculation itself. We use Google Fonts to display the typography on this site.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Your continued use of the service after any change in this Privacy Policy will constitute your acceptance of such change.
