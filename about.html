ABOUT THE PROJECT

This service provides a modern interpretation of Saju Palja (四柱八字), a traditional Eastern astrological system. Our goal is to bridge the gap between ancient wisdom and modern technology, offering clear and insightful analysis of one's destiny.

By inputting your birth date and time, our AI-powered system analyzes the unique combination of your Four Pillars and Eight Characters. We aim to provide not just a prediction, but a tool for self-reflection and understanding, helping you navigate your life's path with greater awareness.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make the profound knowledge of Saju accessible to everyone in a clean, modern, and easy-to-understand interface. We believe that understanding the underlying energies of one's birth chart can empower individuals to make more informed decisions and lead a more harmonious life.
