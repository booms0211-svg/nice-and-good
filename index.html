WHAT IS SAJU?

Saju Palja (四柱八字) is a traditional Eastern astrological system that analyzes a person's destiny based on the four pillars (year, month, day, hour) of their birth. Each pillar consists of two characters, forming the 'eight characters' or 'Palja'. This millennia-old practice, rooted in statistical data and the principles of nature, is used to predict an individual's personality, talents, health, relationships, and the overall flow of their life.

This service utilizes modern AI to analyze traditional Manse-ryeok data, offering a contemporary perspective on your Saju to help you design a better future.
